PROPERTY OPTIMISER — FIRST ALLOCATION INTERACTION
Review date: 26 October 2026
Status: awaiting owner visual and educational comprehension approval.

This page publishes a standalone illustrative frontend experiment, real browser
recordings and rendered screenshots. It is not the full product or a financial
recommendation service. No backend or personal financial data is connected.

The owner rejected the earlier four-stage prototype because it felt like static
financial diagrams with changing labels. The reset asks for one beautiful,
directly manipulable explanatory animation before further stages are attempted.
References: Seeing Theory, The Pudding and Nicky Case. The approved Warm Editorial
language is retained: ivory, forest green, coral, Instrument Sans and a restrained
serif heading. The old house, rectangular savings container and calculator layout
are not retained.

THE INTERACTION
The illustration assumes a GBP 250,000 home and GBP 100,000 in savings.
Each token represents GBP 1,000. Coral tokens are the buyer's savings; green
tokens are mortgage borrowing. Turning the coral handle increases or decreases
the deposit. The same savings tokens travel between retained money and the home.
Mortgage tokens are replaced as savings arrive. Movement reverses immediately
when the gesture reverses. The home price stays constant.

The starting balance is GBP 40,000 deposit, GBP 60,000 retained, GBP 210,000
mortgage. An offline Python Decimal script generates 101 consistent allocations;
the browser selects records and animates geometry. Deposit plus retained money
equals total savings. Deposit plus mortgage equals the purchase price.
There are no rates, repayments, costs, tax, investment returns or eligibility
claims. The extreme allocations are illustrative, not feasible-purchase advice.

The intended eventual product models retained initial investments and monthly
savings made possible by lower mortgage repayments. Neither effect is modelled
in this first allocation interaction. No later educational stage should be
started until the owner approves this one.

HOW TO REVIEW
Open playground/ for the interactive experience.
desktop.webm records real mouse dragging, both directions, rapid reversal, a
transfer button and keyboard interruption of the optional demonstration.
phone.webm records emulated browser touch input and intentional cancellation.
desktop.png and phone.png show the rendered layouts.
money-in-motion.png is a frame extracted from the phone recording.

Keyboard: arrow keys change deposit by GBP 1,000; Page Up / Page Down by
GBP 10,000; Home / End reach endpoints. Transfer buttons provide a non-drag
alternative. Reduced-motion settings select immediate equivalent states.

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS
The source study passed 2 Decimal dataset tests, 58 preview tests, 5 browser
checks, TypeScript and its production build. The affected browser checks were
repeated after final endpoint-copy and preview-server adjustments. These tests
do not prove visual quality or educational comprehension.
Phone testing is browser emulation, not a physical-device test.

REVIEW PROMPT
Review this allocation animation for a novice audience. State which evidence you
actually inspected. Assess whether the same money visibly moves between retained
savings and deposit, and whether its relationship to mortgage borrowing is clear.
Critique direct manipulation, motion choreography, composition, readability and
mobile behaviour. Separate observed issues from hypotheses. Suggest specific
visual improvements to this first interaction only.

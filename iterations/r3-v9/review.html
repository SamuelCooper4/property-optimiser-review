Ten interactive scenes replace twenty mandatory beats. One coherent illustration

£100,000 cash after buying costs = £95,000 allocatable + £5,000 reserve. £400,000 home; default £60,000 deposit and £35,000 opening investment. £2,400 monthly funding covers the mortgage, with the unrounded remainder invested at month end. Fictional fixed-for-term rates: 6.5%, 5%, 4%, at maximum LTVs 90%, 85%, 80%. All bands are reachable.

The investment assumption is a constant 8% annual-effective return, with zero cash return. It is neither a forecast nor a guarantee. No taxes, inflation, fees, refinancing or house-price changes. Exact figures and method are in Details. This illustration does not establish an optimum or change production-engine capabilities.

Calculator limitation

The repository has no supported personal-calculator input route. “Try your own numbers” opens an explicit availability message; it collects no personal information and leaves the completed example intact. This is a truthful handoff boundary, not a working calculator.

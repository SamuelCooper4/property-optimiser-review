R4-v4 connected exploration

Authorised isolated design iteration; agreed brief. React/SVG and the existing browser Python Decimal provider. Production financial behaviour is unchanged.

Calculation and selection contract

A calculation commits one assumptions snapshot. The optimisation horizon, stable best evaluated complete plan, explored deposit/term, and viewed month are separate. Exact selections do not expand the sampled search or change the selected term. The compare operation compares two fixed complete plans at all 481 monthly dates; pair_difference is selected minus reference. Moving the viewed date does not choose a new benchmark.

The joint search evaluates 16 deposit intervals, capital endpoints, exact LTV thresholds and adjacent pennies, and the entered discrete term range. It reports the best evaluated combination, not a proven global optimum. best_by_term is distinct from the joint best; ties_by_term[term][year] contains every exact tie. ridge_by_term has a unique plan only when exactly one eligible plan wins. Purchase-day ties must not be rendered as a unique path.

Financial model

r4-exploratory-v4, effective 2026-11-04, is an isolated illustrative model. It uses 50-significant-digit Decimal calculations, exact-sum accounting, month-end contributions and annual-effective asset returns. GBP display rounding is ROUND_HALF_UP to pence; optional whole-pound diagram labels never feed finance. Price is constant. Entered costs and fees are paid once. Purchase-committed capital is used only for deposit. The initial source/use ledger reconciles savings and committed funds.

Monthly available household funding pays the mortgage, permitted recurring overpayments and monthly-funded events, then invests the remainder, including after payoff. required_payment is contractual; payment is actually funded and capped by the household budget. shortfall and cumulative shortfall_total disclose the difference. Unpaid amounts remain in debt; the plan stays ineligible after its first breach. Such a continuation is an unfunded diagnostic, not a lender arrears/default forecast. At/after the original term, remaining debt plus interest is due. No outside funds are introduced.

monthly_outflow reports the amount of that month's household budget actually consumed by the mortgage, regular overpayments and their charges, and monthly-funded events and their charges. It excludes cash/investment-funded payments and charges. For every payment month, monthly_outflow + contribution = monthly; month zero has no outflow. serial_plan.first_monthly_outflow exposes payment month one's aggregate even when the plan contains annual rows only. This provider aggregate is authoritative for monthly allocation displays: the separate lump and charge fields can combine several funding sources.

opening_cohort contains opening investments and their attributable gains/losses. later_cohort contains subsequent contributions and their attributable gains/losses. Both receive the same return factor; investment-funded withdrawals, including charges, are attributed proportionally to current cohort values before that month's contribution. Their exact sum is investment. The separate contributions/growth decomposition reconciles as opening + cumulative contributions + gains/losses - investment withdrawals. Do not stack the two decompositions together.

principal_total is original mortgage less outstanding debt, including extra principal repayments. repaid_fraction divides it by original mortgage, bounded to 0–1; initial deposit and interest are excluded. Cash purchases report zero with an explicit no-mortgage interpretation. repaid_fraction, applied rate, and assumed_rate serialize without money rounding.

Scenario decisions and reusable context

respond(action="scenario", inputs, original="112500@35", year=30, scenario, context) searches the disclosed deposit grid and tested terms under changed assumptions, adding the exact original complete plan when necessary. An original outside a newly restricted term range is retained as a reference but excluded from winners. When the original is an exact winner tie, it is the displayed best choice and every tied identity is still retained. Supported kinds are investment (return and named stress), rates, household (validated input changes), and overpayment (commitment or optional). Inputs and contexts are never mutated. A valid search returns status="ok" even if no choice is funded. outcome distinguishes same, changed, original unfunded and no funded choice. Unfunded original wealth differences are null; first shortfall, identity and skipped schedule details remain visible.

original_plan and best_plan contain annual rows; original_monthly and best_monthly contain complete serialized monthly plans. Exact ties and discrete choices within GBP5,000 are separate. candidate_count counts purchase choices, schedule_count counts schedule variants; neither certifies a continuous optimum. working_inputs, context, best_context and original_context are replayable. calculate, select, compare and analyse accept the same {stress, overpayment} context. A new scenario preserves unrelated active context. Adopting the winner uses calculate(working_inputs, best_context).

Commitment searches require delivery of the entire entered planned schedule, while mortgage funding is required through the chosen year. Payoff caps and events after redemption do not count as skipped. Optional searches compare the best eligible schedule for every starting purchase choice, including the original: no overpayments at all, the entered schedule, and bounded final-event zero/half/full timing variants. Earlier events and regular payments remain fixed in the other variants. Availability dates clip timing choices; the no-overpayment choice retains the same resources. fixed_plan_comparison separately compares the entered schedule with no extras on the original complete plan, holding context and resources constant.

Exact selection and bounds

bounds takes inputs, whole-year term, year 0-40, optional context and exact penny deposit. It returns separate inclusive penny regions per LTV band, preserving gaps. Capital, reserve, costs, committed funds, LTV limits and required payments through the target year constrain the result. A unit-principal contractual payment envelope gives the analytical cutoff in each homogeneous no-overpayment slice; direct monthly projections verify the actual penny boundary. This avoids per-band monthly bisection. nearest is an exact valid penny amount, or null.

Overpayment sources/allowances can make feasibility nonmonotone within a band. The provider explicitly returns status="unavailable", no continuous regions, discrete funded_choices, and nearest_tested for those contexts. It makes no continuity or global-nearest claim. Exact entry still evaluates the complete schedule directly. select with enforce_funding: true and year returns status="unfunded", explanations and exact nearest or disclosed tested suggestion when unavailable. Omitting enforcement preserves the previous diagnostic selection behavior.

Mortgage-rate paths

The primary v4 interface is fixed_deals: [[duration_months, rate_or_null], ...] plus an explicit continuation_rate. Each duration is independently 24 or 60 months, at most 24 deals and at most 480 total months. The first rate is null and takes each candidate's opening LTV rate. All later rates and continuation are common explicit assumptions in 0-25%. The exact cumulative boundaries of a mixed sequence recast outstanding debt over the remaining original term; the first affected payment is boundary + 1. After the final deal the continuation rate holds. New deals override legacy fixed/path resets. Renewals introduce no fees or property repricing; these are illustrative future assumptions, not forecasts or future-LTV pricing. Zero debt remains zero.

Inputs accept rate_path: [[elapsed_month, decimal_annual_rate], ...] and renewal_months (default 60). At most 24 distinct ascending change points, elapsed months 0–480 and rates 0–25%, are supported. The opening LTV product rate applies through fixed_months. At elapsed fixed_months, then every renewal_months, a path-enabled mortgage adopts the most recent available rate. The first payment after that elapsed date uses the new rate. Changes between renewals update assumed_rate only. Each renewal recalculates the annuity on outstanding debt over the original remaining term, without changing the final contractual maturity.

Before the first path point, the explicit legacy post_rate, if entered, is the available-rate fallback; otherwise the initial product rate is used. With no path, post_rate preserves the former single reset. With neither, no renewal/recalculation is performed. A fixed period of zero applies an available rate immediately. Renewals after payoff do not create debt. rate is applied mortgage pricing; assumed_rate is the external rate available at the beginning of that payment month. renewal identifies scheduled reset months.

analyse may accept a scenario options.rate_path, renewal_months and fixed_months; an explicitly supplied legacy path replaces active fixed deals for that rate analysis only. Without an explicit path, scalar options.rate replaces every future fixed-deal rate and continuation while preserving the opening deal and each duration; without fixed deals it retains the legacy single-reset behavior. The rate sensitivity adds two percentage points to every future deal and continuation, preserving opening pricing. Both fixed plans receive the same external scenario. Base and term comparisons retain the adopted assumptions. Committing the path through calculate is the separate action that searches under it.

Term comparisons

term_comparison holds deposit constant. When the explored term equals options.recommended_term (otherwise the reference plan's term), it automatically selects the nearest shorter funded tested term. An alternative explored term compares with the recommended term if funded. With no available reference, status="no_shorter", reference_available=false, a reason and the monthly constraint are explicit; reference is null. Otherwise it compares the explored and reference terms. It provides monthly selected/reference plans, pair differences, and horizon investment/cash/debt/net differences. Net difference = investment difference + cash difference - remaining-debt difference. Interest is not deducted again. selected_interest and reference_interest cover the same common_period_months; each plan's full projection interest is a separate value.

The annual-return comparison evaluates -4% through 12% in one-percentage-point steps. The tested rate replaces the scalar no-growth (low_return) context for this sweep; named shock/recovery paths and adopted overpayment settings remain active. Mortgage funding and full adopted-schedule delivery are both required before reporting a comparison or reversal bracket. Named stress and sensitivity comparisons likewise return eligible=false and a null difference when an adopted commitment cannot be delivered. Diagnostic plans still report the skipped payments. break_even_brackets reports strictly opposite-sign neighbouring eligible samples, not a precise root; exact zero samples remain visible in returns. At the default £112,500 deposit, a 30-year versus 35-year comparison at year 30 reproduces about £14,185 in favour of 35 years and a ranking-change interval of 4–5%. This is assumption-dependent, not a recommended mortgage product. The longest entered/evaluated term is explicit.

Overpayments and timing search

Supported settings: regular, regular_start, regular_end (inclusive payment months, 1–480), events: [{month, amount, source, available_month}], treatment (term or payment), allowance, and charge_rate. At most 24 events are supported. Legacy lump, month, source, and available_month remain supported. Regular payments use monthly surplus. Event sources are monthly, investment, or cash; cash withdrawals may use only the balance above the original nominal reserve. A negative cash return can itself reduce cash below that reserve; the reserve rule prevents withdrawals, not market losses.

Events before their availability dates or beyond the selected source balance are skipped with explicit issues and event results. Funds are existing assets or the same monthly household budget, never additional contributions. Multiple same-month events apply in entered order after the regular overpayment and before investment contributions. An event is capped at remaining debt. events results identify requested amount/date/source/availability plus actual paid, charge, and issue.

The annual allowance is a fraction of the debt at the start of each purchase-anniversary year. Regular payments and events consume it together; the excess incurs charge_rate. The selected source must fund principal plus charge. term treatment retains the contractual payment between renewals and may repay earlier; payment recalculates the following payment over the unchanged remaining original term. Renewals recalculate the remaining-term annuity in both cases. No lender-specific ERC rules, annual resets at calendar year-end, new product fees or remortgage costs are implied.

search_requested: false evaluates the entered strategy without timing candidates; true requests the search (omitting the flag retains legacy search behaviour). Timing search normalizes explicit events and a simultaneous legacy lump into one chronological list, preserving entered order for same-month payments. It varies only the latest chronological event, holding every other event and recurring payment fixed. Supporting timing analysis and optional purchase search use the same construction; timing rows include their complete candidate overpayment settings. It evaluates zero once, then half/full entered amount at the availability-clipped window start, midpoint and end; duplicate schedules collapse. If availability is after the window only the zero case remains. Zero retains the money in its source, rather than removing resources. Candidates with skipped events/regular payments or funding shortfalls are ineligible. Exact Decimal values determine ranking/ties before display rounding. search_scope describes these bounds; this is not a general optimum over all schedules. interest_saving uses the selected common horizon.

Limits and verification

No tax/LISA eligibility, investment fees/tax, inflation, changing property values, lender-specific payment rounding, affordability underwriting, suitability, probabilistic forecasts or validated production-successor claim. Deterministic loss/recovery paths expose their shock/recovery dates and assumptions. The introduction remains a separate fixed example.

Run the isolated provider suite with python -m unittest discover -s design/r4-v4 -p test_*.py from the repository root. It includes independent 100-digit mixed-deal closed forms and annuity/asset references, Fraction-derived penny payment cutoffs, nonmonotone LTV gaps, scenario outcomes/context adoption and optional/commitment eligibility, legacy reset regression, no-reset regression, source/cohort conservation, threshold/availability/regular-window boundaries, shortfall resource accounting, fixed-plan comparisons, exact ties and a zero-return timing-search reference. Browser parity and integration, full backend gates and publication are recorded separately by the iteration workflow.

Calculation and selection contract

A calculation commits one assumptions snapshot. The optimisation horizon, stable best evaluated complete plan, explored deposit/term, and viewed month are separate. Exact selections do not expand the sampled search or change the selected term. The compare operation compares two fixed complete plans at all 481 monthly dates; pair_difference is selected minus reference. Moving the viewed date does not choose a new benchmark.

The joint search evaluates 16 deposit intervals, capital endpoints, exact LTV thresholds and adjacent pennies, and the entered discrete term range. It reports the best evaluated combination, not a proven global optimum. best_by_term is distinct from the joint best; ties_by_term[term][year] contains every exact tie. ridge_by_term has a unique plan only when exactly one eligible plan wins. Purchase-day ties must not be rendered as a unique path.

Financial model

r4-exploratory-v3, effective 2026-11-03, is an isolated illustrative model. It uses 50-significant-digit Decimal calculations, exact-sum accounting, month-end contributions and annual-effective asset returns. GBP display rounding is ROUND_HALF_UP to pence; optional whole-pound diagram labels never feed finance. Price is constant. Entered costs and fees are paid once. Purchase-committed capital is used only for deposit. The initial source/use ledger reconciles savings and committed funds.

Monthly available household funding pays the mortgage, permitted recurring overpayments and monthly-funded events, then invests the remainder, including after payoff. required_payment is contractual; payment is actually funded and capped by the household budget. shortfall and cumulative shortfall_total disclose the difference. Unpaid amounts remain in debt; the plan stays ineligible after its first breach. Such a continuation is an unfunded diagnostic, not a lender arrears/default forecast. At/after the original term, remaining debt plus interest is due. No outside funds are introduced.

opening_cohort contains opening investments and their attributable gains/losses. later_cohort contains subsequent contributions and their attributable gains/losses. Both receive the same return factor; investment-funded withdrawals, including charges, are attributed proportionally to current cohort values before that month's contribution. Their exact sum is investment. The separate contributions/growth decomposition reconciles as opening + cumulative contributions + gains/losses - investment withdrawals. Do not stack the two decompositions together.

principal_total is original mortgage less outstanding debt, including extra principal repayments. repaid_fraction divides it by original mortgage, bounded to 0�1; initial deposit and interest are excluded. Cash purchases report zero with an explicit no-mortgage interpretation. repaid_fraction, applied rate, and assumed_rate serialize without money rounding.

Mortgage-rate paths

Inputs accept rate_path: [[elapsed_month, decimal_annual_rate], ...] and renewal_months (default 60). At most 24 distinct ascending change points, elapsed months 0�480 and rates 0�25%, are supported. The opening LTV product rate applies through fixed_months. At elapsed fixed_months, then every renewal_months, a path-enabled mortgage adopts the most recent available rate. The first payment after that elapsed date uses the new rate. Changes between renewals update assumed_rate only. Each renewal recalculates the annuity on outstanding debt over the original remaining term, without changing the final contractual maturity.

Before the first path point, the explicit legacy post_rate, if entered, is the available-rate fallback; otherwise the initial product rate is used. With no path, post_rate preserves the former single reset. With neither, no renewal/recalculation is performed. A fixed period of zero applies an available rate immediately. Renewals after payoff do not create debt. rate is applied mortgage pricing; assumed_rate is the external rate available at the beginning of that payment month. renewal identifies scheduled reset months.

analyse may accept a scenario options.rate_path, renewal_months and fixed_months; absent a scenario path, its legacy scalar options.rate tests a single reset. Both fixed plans receive the same external scenario. Committing the path through calculate is the separate action that searches under it.

Term comparisons

term_comparison holds deposit constant and compares the explored term with options.recommended_term (otherwise the reference plan's term). It provides monthly selected/reference plans, pair differences, and horizon investment/cash/debt/net differences. Net difference = investment difference + cash difference - remaining-debt difference. Interest is not deducted again. selected_interest and reference_interest cover the same common_period_months; each plan's full projection interest is a separate value.

The annual-return comparison evaluates -4% through 12% in one-percentage-point steps. break_even_brackets reports strictly opposite-sign neighbouring samples, not a precise root; exact zero samples remain visible in returns. At the default �112,500 deposit, a 30-year versus 35-year comparison at year 30 reproduces about �14,185 in favour of 35 years and a ranking-change interval of 4�5%. This is assumption-dependent, not a recommended mortgage product. The longest entered/evaluated term is explicit.

Overpayments and timing search

Supported settings: regular, regular_start, regular_end (inclusive payment months, 1�480), events: [{month, amount, source, available_month}], treatment (term or payment), allowance, and charge_rate. At most 24 events are supported. Legacy lump, month, source, and available_month remain supported. Regular payments use monthly surplus. Event sources are monthly, investment, or cash; cash withdrawals may use only the balance above the original nominal reserve. A negative cash return can itself reduce cash below that reserve; the reserve rule prevents withdrawals, not market losses.

Events before their availability dates or beyond the selected source balance are skipped with explicit issues and event results. Funds are existing assets or the same monthly household budget, never additional contributions. Multiple same-month events apply in entered order after the regular overpayment and before investment contributions. An event is capped at remaining debt. events results identify requested amount/date/source/availability plus actual paid, charge, and issue.

The annual allowance is a fraction of the debt at the start of each purchase-anniversary year. Regular payments and events consume it together; the excess incurs charge_rate. The selected source must fund principal plus charge. term treatment retains the contractual payment between renewals and may repay earlier; payment recalculates the following payment over the unchanged remaining original term. Renewals recalculate the remaining-term annuity in both cases. No lender-specific ERC rules, annual resets at calendar year-end, new product fees or remortgage costs are implied.

search_requested: false evaluates the entered strategy without timing candidates; true requests the search (omitting the flag retains legacy search behaviour). Timing search varies only the final listed event (or legacy lump), holding earlier events and recurring payments fixed. It evaluates zero once, then half/full entered amount at the availability-clipped window start, midpoint and end; duplicate schedules collapse. If availability is after the window only the zero case remains. Zero retains the money in its source, rather than removing resources. Candidates with skipped events/regular payments or funding shortfalls are ineligible. Exact Decimal values determine ranking/ties before display rounding. search_scope describes these bounds; this is not a general optimum over all schedules. interest_saving uses the selected common horizon.

Limits and verification

No tax/LISA eligibility, investment fees/tax, inflation, changing property values, lender-specific payment rounding, affordability underwriting, suitability, probabilistic forecasts or validated production-successor claim. Deterministic loss/recovery paths expose their shock/recovery dates and assumptions. The introduction remains a separate fixed example.

Run the isolated provider suite with python -m unittest discover -s design/r4-v3 -p test_*.py from the repository root. It includes independent 100-digit annuity/asset references, legacy reset regression, no-reset regression, source/cohort conservation, threshold/availability/regular-window boundaries, shortfall resource accounting, fixed-plan comparisons, exact ties and a zero-return timing-search reference. Browser parity and integration, full backend gates and publication are recorded separately by the iteration workflow.

A bigger or smaller house deposit?

See how the amount put towards a house deposit changes a fictional household's mortgage, monthly payments and savings left to invest.

A larger deposit reduces borrowing and may change the mortgage rate. A smaller deposit leaves more money available at the start. This example compares both choices over 30 years using the same household and stated assumptions.

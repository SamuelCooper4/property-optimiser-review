  • Growth scale and two investment cohorts are established before playback. Actual principal repayment fills the house.
  • Two five-second runs, with the first result kept visible throughout the second. Both totals use the same scale.

Tax, inflation, fees, refinancing, overpayments, house-price changes and probabilities are omitted. Growth is not guaranteed. Two examples do not establish the global optimum or a personal recommendation. The production financial engine is unchanged; this isolated Python Decimal provider is a bounded illustration.

Try a novice comprehension check

After viewing, ask without prompting:

  1. What are the two parts of a monthly payment? Which reduces the loan?
  2. What changed at the deposit threshold, beyond borrowing less?
  3. What does the lighter investment colour contain? Does it include gains or losses?
  4. Why did this smaller deposit finish ahead despite the larger monthly investment in the other example?
  5. Does this prove the best deposit for you? What belongs in your own calculator?

Record misunderstandings before explaining. Physical-device and novice-comprehension testing are unperformed. Browser emulation and agent inspection do not establish owner acceptance.

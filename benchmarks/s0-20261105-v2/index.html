1. Test conditions

Use Chrome, keep this page visible and use Wi-Fi. Switching apps invalidates automated timing; restart if that happens. Close other demanding apps. The automated checks take several minutes; the first load downloads about 12 MB. New worker starts may reuse the browser’s network cache.

2. Automated checks

30 fresh worker starts, 100 warm operations per primary workload, 30-second uncached input sequences, heavy-work priority, cancellation and recovery. These generated inputs are recorded separately from your touch test. Timing observes DOM and animation frames; it is not optical video evidence.
